What do you want help with?

Six goals, three short lessons each, about 10 to 15 minutes a lesson. Start with any one.

How a lesson works

No fake terminal. You learn from a real session, then do the real thing.

1

Sit in the human's seat

Pick or type what to say to Claude, and answer when it asks permission. Claude replies to you.

2

Watch your files change

Beside the chat, see what the session is doing to the folder, sheet or page as it happens.

3

Try it yourself

Run a ready prompt in your own Claude, then compare with what we got.

What you'll be able to do

Each goal gives you one real skill. Your progress stays in this browser. No account, no streaks.

Ready for code?

The Advanced track is for people who build software, in the terminal.

From issue to pull request

Plan mode, CLAUDE.md for a codebase, tests and review.

Delegate a big change

Subagents and parallel work on a real refactor.

Automate your repo

Hooks, headless runs and GitHub Actions.

Before you start: you'll need a paid Claude plan (Pro or higher) to try things yourself. The lessons are free.
