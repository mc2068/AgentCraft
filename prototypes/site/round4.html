QUESTIONS

Good to know

Do I need to know how to code?

No. The Foundations track is for people who've never written code. You work with folders, documents, spreadsheets and simple web pages.

Is it free? What do I need?

The lessons are free. To try things in your own Claude you need a paid Claude plan (Pro or higher) and the Claude desktop app.

Is this made by Anthropic?

No. Agentcraft is an independent, unofficial guide. "Claude" and "Claude Code" are trademarks of Anthropic.

How do you keep lessons up to date?

Every lesson is recorded from a real session and shows which Claude Code version and date it was checked against. When Claude Code changes, we record it again.

Will Claude touch my files during a lesson?

No. Lessons run in your browser with sample files. You only use your own files when you choose to try it yourself.
